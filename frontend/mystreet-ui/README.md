# MyStreet Shoppers UI

This storefront gives customers a simple place to browse sneakers, manage a cart, and complete an order.

## Customer features

- Browse products and featured collections
- Open product details and choose a size
- Add products to the cart and update quantities
- Sign in to keep track of orders and account activity
- Review past purchases from the customer area

## Experience goals

- Keep shopping steps short and clear
- Present products with a clean retail layout
- Make cart and checkout feel lightweight
